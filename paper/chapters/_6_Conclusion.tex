\chapter{Conclusion}
This WAB comapred representative top-down, bottom-up and incremental BVH implementations on the same 900 static datasets.

For the tested variants, construction strongly affected broad-phase checks.
Bottom-up produced the lowest mean in 17 of 18 parameter combinations. Top-down produced the highest in all 18.
Incremental generally ranked between them.

Primitive-check counts were identical due to fixed leaf AABBs and traversal producing the same test candidates.

The conclusion is restricted to these heuristics and scenes, other construction methods, or scene behaviours could change the ranking.
