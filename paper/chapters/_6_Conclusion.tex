\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
This WAB compared representative top-down, bottom-up and incremental BVH implementations on the same 900 static datasets.

The research question can be answered as follows:
The evaluated construction algorithm influenced the number of bounding-volume checks required during traversal
Bottom-up produced the lowest mean in 15 of 18 parameter combinations. 
Top-down produced the highest in all 18.
Incremental generally ranked between them.
Primitive-check counts were identical due to fixed leaf AABBs and traversal producing the same test candidates.

The results are restricted to these heuristics and scenes, therefore they do not establish a general ranking of the three construction classes.

Future work could extend the experiment with additional construction heuristics, in particular SAH-based top-down construction, different scene distributions and dynamic scenarios.
Including measurements on construction cost would further allow evaluating the trade-off between construction cost and traversal performance.
