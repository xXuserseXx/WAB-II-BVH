\chapter{Methodology}

To investigate the influence of the construction strategy on the quality of the BVH, an experiment is implemented. 
Three algorithms are to be implemented, those being: top-down, bottom-up, and incremental, following the classification presented by Ericson\cite{ericson2004real}
The experiment will focus on static two-dimensional collision detection, therefore particles will not move after dataset generation, and each hierarchy is generated completly before traversal is done.

The independet variable of the experiment will be the BVH construction strategy, the dependent variables are the number of bounding volume and primitive collision checks performed during traversal.
A bounding volume collision check is defined as a collision check where one of the aabb in the check contains more than one primitive particle.

To isolate the effect of the construction strategy, all other components of the collision detecion procedure are kept constant between experiment runs.
All strategies receive the same datasets and use the same geometric representation, collision detection and traversal procedure. 
This is meant to assure, that differences in the recorded number of checks can primarly be attributed to the differences in the structure generated by the respective construction algorithms.
